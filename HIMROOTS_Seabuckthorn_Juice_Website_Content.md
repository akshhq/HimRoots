## 1. Discover the Power of the Golden Berry

Seabuckthorn (*Hippophae rhamnoides*) is a resilient shrub that grows in
mountainous regions, including the Himalayan landscape. Its bright
orange berries have long been valued in traditional food practices and
are increasingly studied for their diverse nutritional and phytochemical
composition.

Within these small berries lies an impressive combination of naturally
occurring compounds, including vitamin C, carotenoids, tocopherols,
flavonoids, organic acids and plant lipids.

Seabuckthorn is particularly interesting because its seeds and fruit
pulp have different fatty-acid compositions. The seeds contain Omega-3
and Omega-6 fatty acids, while the pulp oil is notable for its Omega-7
content.

This natural diversity makes seabuckthorn a distinctive addition to a
balanced, plant-focused diet.

## 2. The Omega 3-6-7-9 Profile: Four Fatty Acids, One Extraordinary Fruit

Seabuckthorn stands apart from ordinary fruits because of the natural
fatty acids found in its seed and pulp oils.

Its distinctive lipid composition includes two essential polyunsaturated
fatty acids and two monounsaturated fatty acids. Each has a different
structure and role in human nutrition.

### Ω3 --- Omega-3: Alpha-Linolenic Acid (ALA)

**Essential fatty acid**

Omega-3 is a family of polyunsaturated fatty acids. Seabuckthorn seed
oil contains alpha-linolenic acid (ALA), a plant-based Omega-3 that the
human body cannot produce on its own.

ALA contributes to normal growth and development and is an important
component of a nutritionally balanced diet. The body can convert a
limited proportion of ALA into the longer-chain fatty acids EPA and DHA,
although this conversion is generally low.

Seabuckthorn seed oil provides a botanical source of ALA, making the
berry's seed fraction particularly interesting for plant-based
nutrition.

### Ω6 --- Omega-6: Linoleic Acid (LA)

**Essential fatty acid**

Omega-6 is another essential fatty-acid family, with linoleic acid being
its principal dietary representative.

Linoleic acid contributes to the structure of cell membranes and is
involved in maintaining the skin's normal barrier function. It also
serves as a precursor for other biologically active compounds.

Seabuckthorn seed oil naturally contains both linoleic acid and
alpha-linolenic acid, offering an interesting combination of the two
essential fatty acids.

### Ω7 --- Omega-7: Palmitoleic Acid

**The signature fatty acid**

Omega-7 is one of the most distinctive features of seabuckthorn. Its
principal representative in the berry is palmitoleic acid, a
monounsaturated fatty acid found in comparatively few common plant-based
food oils.

Unlike Omega-3 and Omega-6, Omega-7 is not classified as an essential
fatty acid because the human body can synthesize it.

Palmitoleic acid is a component of human lipids and has attracted
scientific interest for its possible involvement in skin physiology,
mucosal health and lipid metabolism.

Seabuckthorn pulp oil is especially notable for its palmitoleic acid
content. However, research into the specific clinical benefits of
Omega-7 is ongoing, and consuming seabuckthorn juice should not be
presented as a treatment for skin or metabolic disorders.

### Ω9 --- Omega-9: Oleic Acid

**Monounsaturated fatty acid**

Omega-9 fatty acids include oleic acid, a monounsaturated fat also found
in foods such as olive oil, avocados and nuts.

Oleic acid is involved in normal cell-membrane structure and forms part
of dietary fat intake. When unsaturated fats replace saturated fats in
the diet, they can contribute to a more favorable dietary fatty-acid
balance.

Seabuckthorn naturally contains oleic acid in both its seed and pulp
oils, adding another dimension to its diverse botanical lipid
composition.

## 3. Beyond Omegas: A Spectrum of Natural Nutrients

The nutritional story of seabuckthorn goes far beyond its fatty-acid
composition. The berry also contains a range of water-soluble and
fat-soluble compounds that contribute to its distinctive colour, flavour
and nutritional character.

### Vitamin C --- Natural Nutritional Support

Seabuckthorn berries are known for their naturally occurring vitamin C.
Vitamin C contributes to normal immune-system function, collagen
formation and protection of cells from oxidative stress.

The amount retained in juice depends on processing, storage and
formulation.

### Vitamin E --- A Fat-Soluble Antioxidant

Seabuckthorn oils contain tocopherols, compounds belonging to the
vitamin E family. Vitamin E contributes to the protection of cells from
oxidative stress.

Its concentration in the finished beverage depends on the amount of
berry oil retained.

### Carotenoids --- Nature's Golden Pigments

Seabuckthorn contains carotenoids, the naturally occurring pigments
responsible for much of its vibrant orange colour.

These compounds are studied for their antioxidant properties, and
certain carotenoids can be converted by the body into vitamin A.

### Flavonoids and Polyphenols --- Botanical Diversity

Seabuckthorn contains naturally occurring polyphenolic compounds,
including flavonoids. These plant compounds are of scientific interest
because of their antioxidant activity and potential roles in human
nutrition.

These nutrients and compounds have been identified in seabuckthorn fruit
and its fractions. Their levels in HIMROOTS WELLNESS juice must be
confirmed through finished-product testing before making specific
nutrient-content claims.

The seabuckthorn fruit contains all four omega families, but the amount

### What is special about Omega-7?

Omega-7, particularly palmitoleic acid, is a monounsaturated fatty acid
found in seabuckthorn pulp oil. It is relatively uncommon in widely
consumed plant oils and is being studied for its biological properties.

### Can seabuckthorn juice replace fish oil or Omega-3 supplements?

No direct equivalence should be assumed. Seabuckthorn provides
plant-based ALA, whereas fish oil typically provides EPA and DHA. Their
compositions differ, and ordinary juice may contain only small
quantities of fatty acids.

### Can I drink seabuckthorn juice every day?

Seabuckthorn juice may be enjoyed as part of a balanced diet, subject to
the finished product's ingredients and serving instructions. People who
are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication or managing a medical
condition should seek professional advice before regularly consuming
concentrated herbal blends.

### Why is seabuckthorn juice naturally tangy?

Seabuckthorn contains naturally occurring organic acids that contribute
to its distinctive tart flavour. Its taste may vary with fruit variety,
ripeness and formulation.
